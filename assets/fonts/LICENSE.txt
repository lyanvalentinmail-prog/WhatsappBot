DejaVu fonts (DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf)
-----------------------------------
Bundled here so that `.brat` can render text locally with FFmpeg without
depending on whichever fonts (if any) happen to be installed on the
device running the bot (important for Termux, which does not ship
fonts by default).

DejaVu fonts are built on top of the Bitstream Vera fonts and are
released under a permissive free license that allows embedding,
modification and redistribution (including commercial use), with
attribution preserved. Full license text and font authors:
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
